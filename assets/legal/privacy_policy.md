# Privacy Policy — Student Guide

**Last updated:** 1 September 2026  
**Developer:** Audrey  
**Package:** com.audrey.student.guide

Student Guide is built so you can plan classes, assignments, notes, grades, and study sessions on your own device.

## Data we collect

Student Guide does **not** collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.

All information you enter (your name, school, subjects, tasks, notes, grades, flashcards, and study sessions) is stored **only on your device** using the operating system’s local storage.

## Permissions

The app does not require internet access, location, camera, contacts, or microphone permission to work.

If you use **Export backup**, a JSON copy of your local data is created so you can save or share it yourself. We never receive that file.

## Children’s privacy

The app is meant for students aged 13 and older. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data deletion

You can erase everything from **More → Settings → Clear all data**. Uninstalling the app also removes local data unless your device backup restored it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app copy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email listed on the Google Play store listing for Student Guide.
