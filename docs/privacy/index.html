Privacy Policy

Student Guide by Audrey
Android package: com.audrey.student.guide
Last updated: 1 September 2026

Student Guide is a student planner. You can organize classes, assignments, exams, notes, grades, flashcards, and study sessions on your own device.

Data we collect

Student Guide does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not create accounts, show ads, or include analytics or tracking SDKs.

Anything you type (such as your name, school, subjects, tasks, notes, grades, flashcards, and study history) is stored only on your device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences). That information is never sent to Audrey or to any server.

Permissions

The app does not need internet, location, camera, contacts, microphone, or advertising ID permission to work.

If you use Export backup, the app creates a JSON copy of the data on your device so you can save or share it yourself (for example through the Android share sheet). We do not receive that file.

Children’s privacy

The app is meant for students aged 13 and older. Because we do not collect personal data from the device, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data deletion

You can erase local data in the app: More → Settings → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes this data unless your device backup restored it later.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the copy inside the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email shown on the Google Play listing for Student Guide (com.audrey.student.guide).
